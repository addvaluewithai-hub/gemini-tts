# englishlive A2 listening recordings

24 two-voice example conversations for the englishlive (Englotti) A2 Core speaking lessons, one factory job each.
Same layout and rules as `../englishlive-b1-listening/`:

- `jobs/<slug>.json`: the exact `tts.generate` client payload (`job_id` + `request`) plus the lesson it belongs to.
  Generated by `scripts/audio/build-listening-jobs.ts --level A2` in englishlive from the lesson's canonical dialogue
  (speaking-practice A2 Core, grounded in english-course A2); do not edit by hand. Every request passes
  `normalizeRequest()`.
- `campaign.json`: every job with its word count, SHA-256 of the exact lines and voices, in issues of at most five.
- `results/<job_id>.json` and `results/<job_id>.transcript.json`: the factory's result and transcript for each
  delivered job.

Settings for every job: `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`, no model fallback, two speakers (Partner and Learner) cast per
lesson, WAV 24 kHz, en-US word transcript and VTT. Every turn's style ends with the A2 delivery: American English
accent, slow and clear pace for A2 learners, a short pause between sentences. About 2,400 words in total, 82-124
words per conversation.

Paid synthesis needs the owner's explicit approval per issue.
