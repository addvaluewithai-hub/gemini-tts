# Film pilot 01: the coin that never moves

A from-scratch test of a different way to make an episode: the idea is acted out as a short silent-film scene
(people who want something, something in the way, a result), and the host says as little as possible over it.
The test for the scene: with the sound off, the point and the joke still land.

The scene (about 35 seconds): Ali holds out a coin to Mona at a street cafe; a banker's hand stops it; the banker
rubs out a number in his book and writes another; the coin never leaves the table; the host bites it to check;
the banker walks off with the book and every head follows it.

`request.json` is the voice (five short lines). The scene itself is drawn in `video/film/`.
