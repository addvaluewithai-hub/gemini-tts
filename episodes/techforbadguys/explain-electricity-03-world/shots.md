# Electricity part 3 of 4 (why the lights go out), world picture: the shot list

Same world, props and rules as parts 1 and 2 (`../explain-electricity-01-world/shots.md`): the file's teal-and-navy
night (`P` from `sets/elec.js`), the teal and orange houses, pylons, the 50 Hz gauge from part 2's balance. Nothing in
Arabic on screen; numbers, years, names in Latin letters (OHIO, NEW YORK, INDIA, TEXAS, SPAIN, PORTUGAL, QUEBEC,
UKRAINE), symbols. No people: crowds are lit windows and empty streets; the workers carrying a pole are drawn as poles on
stands that tip (no figures). Deaths are stated by the voice only: on screen a number on a quiet card, no bodies, no
suffering. The Texas section (music down, calm) is still and cold: snow, dark windows, a clock.

The thing that travels: **the grid of dots and lines** (stations as gold dots, cities as lit clusters, lines between
them); a failure is a line going red then dark, and the darkness spreading as a wave.
