Same world, props and rules as parts 1–3 (`../explain-electricity-01-world/shots.md`): the file's teal-and-navy night
(`P` from `sets/elec.js`), the teal and orange houses, pylons, part 2's balance and its 50 Hz gauge, part 3's grid of

studies is a lit desk with an open book and a kerosene lamp, no figure; crowds are lit windows. Peek leans in on beats

wall with lithium as small gold dots climbing a ladder); **the duck curve** (a demand line across a day sky that is
drawn as a duck: low belly at noon, steep neck at sunset); **the sun** (gold, from part 2) feeding panels.
