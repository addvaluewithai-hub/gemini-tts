# Crypto part 3, world picture: the shot list

One wordless shot per board beat (`video/sets/c3<scene>.js`), drawn in the world of parts 1 and 2 (`sets/kit.js`,
`bank.js`, `props.js`, `c2props.js`, and part 3's props in `sets/c3props.js`). Host beats are unchanged; `host
left/right` keeps that lower corner clear. Times come from the Vertex take (`node sets/beats.cjs`) and each action
sits on its word; until that take exists the times below are the words to aim at, not seconds.

Things that travel through the episode: **the key** (a gold key whose teeth are a long number; one per house colour),
**the address** (a mailbox slot with a long code `bc1q…`, the house colour on its flag), **the stamp** (the key's
signature: a seal that leaves a different mark on each page but one the network can check), **the book** (the public
ledger from parts 1 and 2, with its laptops), **the paper with twelve words** (twelve short bars on a cream slip, never
readable words), **the hot wallet** (a phone with a wifi arc) and **the cold wallet** (a small steel box with no
antenna), **the exchange** (a glass tower with a coin sign; it plays the bank's role from part 1), **the receipt**
(a paper slip the exchange hands back instead of coins), **the dump** (Newport landfill), **the flash drive** with
attempt dots, **the popcorn tin**, the people as houses in their colours, every date on the calendar page.
