Facts are stated from general knowledge and from web research on 12 November 2026 (see explain-crypto-03-ar/plan.md for links), not from pages captured for this episode: Ethereum was proposed by Vitalik Buterin in late 2013 at 19 and launched in July 2015; in June 2016 about 60 million dollars was drained from The DAO through a flaw in its contract and the chain was forked to undo it; Ethereum moved to proof of stake on 15 September 2022 and its electricity use fell by more than 99 percent; the 2017 token sales raised billions and one of them, EOS, about 4 billion dollars; a validator stakes 32 ether; Tether is among the largest holders of US Treasury bills; more than 3 billion dollars was stolen from crypto protocols in 2022, most of it from DeFi (Chainalysis); stablecoin supply was about 300 billion dollars in October 2026; Tether said it froze about 1.5 billion dollars of USDT linked to the October 2026 Binance theft within about half an hour; Terra's stablecoin and its sister coin lost about 40 billion dollars in a week in May 2022 and its founder was sentenced to 15 years in December 2025; the US stablecoin law of July 2025 requires one-to-one reserves; a digital artwork sold for 69 million dollars in March 2021; the first tweet sold as an NFT for 2.9 million dollars in March 2021 and drew a top bid of about 280 dollars when relisted in April 2022; a 2023 study by dappGambl found 95 percent of NFT collections had no market value; Dogecoin was created in December 2013 as a joke, was worth close to 90 billion dollars in May 2021, and co-creator Billy Markus has said he sold his holdings in 2015 for about the price of a used car;
