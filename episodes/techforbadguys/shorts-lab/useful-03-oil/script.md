# الزيت ولّع في الطاسة؟ المية عمرها ما تطفّيه 🔥 #Shorts

**English title:** Oil on fire in the pan? Water will never put it out

**What the viewer can do after watching:** never throw water on burning oil and never carry the pan; turn off the heat if it is safe, cover the pan with a lid or a tray and leave it where it is; if the fire grows, get everyone out, close the kitchen door and call the fire service.

Format: useful Short 03. The drawn scene fills the screen; Peek has three full-screen studio shots (lines 1, 5, 10).

## Narration

1. يا فندم، لما الزيت بيولّع في الطاسة، أول حاجة البشري بيعملها إنه يرمي عليه مية.. ودي أسوأ حاجة ممكن يعملها.
   [PEEK: studio, full screen. He holds a glass of water at arm's length and eyes it with suspicion]
2. الزيت المولّع سخن أكتر بكتير من درجة غليان المية.
   [SCENE: a man in a home kitchen; a frying pan in flames on the stove; he runs to the tap and fills a cup]
3. فالمية أول ما تلمسه بتبقى بخار في لحظة.. والبخار بيرمي الزيت المولّع في كل حتة.
   [SCENE: slow motion: the water hits the oil, flashes to steam, burning drops fly outward]
4. المطافي بتسمّيها: كورة نار.
   [SCENE: a fireball shoots up to the kitchen ceiling; the man thrown back, eyebrows smoking]
5. من كوباية مية يا فندم.
   [PEEK: studio, reaction. He puts the glass of water down very slowly and slides it away with one finger]
6. والغلطة التانية: إنه يشيل الطاسة ويجري بيها. الزيت بيتدلق عليه، والنار بتمشي معاه.
   [SCENE: the man carrying the flaming pan toward the door, burning oil sloshing over the edge, flames trailing; then a big cross over the picture]
7. الصح: اقفل النار لو تقدر بأمان. غطّي الطاسة بغطا أو صينية. وسيبها مكانها.
   [SCENE: three beats: a hand turns the stove knob off; a lid slides over the pan from the side; the flames die under the lid, the pan untouched on the stove]
8. ولو النار كبرت: اخرجوا كلكم، اقفلوا باب المطبخ، واتصلوا بالمطافي.
   [SCENE: the family walking out, the kitchen door closed behind them; the man on the phone on the stairs]
9. يعني: لا مية.. ولا تشيلها. اقفل، غطّي، وابعد.
   [SCENE: five clear icons in a row: a water cup crossed out, a carried pan crossed out, a knob turned off, a lid on a pan, a person stepping back]
10. البشر اتعلموا من صغرهم إن المية بتطفّي النار.. ومحدش قال لهم «إلا دي». <short pause> Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.
   [PEEK: studio, he places a pan lid on the desk like a medal, deadpan; then sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell]

## Sources

- "Don't tackle the fire yourself and don't try to move the pan. Never throw water over a fire as it could create a fireball. Turn off the heat, to stop the fire from growing, but only if it's safe to do so. Leave the room, close the door, warn others, and call 999" (London Fire Brigade): https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/cooking/pan-fires/
- "When you add water to a burning chip pan the oil is hotter than the boiling point of water. Therefore, the water is superheated and expands rapidly as steam. This then splashes, spatters, and vaporises, carrying burning oil to create a huge fireball"; "Never try to carry the burning pan of oil as this can lead to serious burns and further fire spread"; "NEVER use water on the fire" (Nottinghamshire Fire and Rescue Service): https://www.notts-fire.gov.uk/news/warnings-issued-following-chip-pan-fire
- "Keep a pan lid or baking sheet nearby. Use it to cover the pan if it catches on fire. This will put out the fire" (U.S. Fire Administration, FEMA): https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prevent-fires/cooking/
- **The sources differ on one point, and the script shows both:** the U.S. Fire Administration tells people to cover the pan with a lid; the two UK fire services say do not tackle it yourself, turn the heat off if safe, get out and call. All three agree on no water and not moving the pan. So line 7 gives the lid (with «لو تقدر بأمان») and line 8 gives the UK rule for a fire that grows. If the manager prefers only the stricter UK advice, line 7 becomes «اقفل النار لو تقدر بأمان، وسيب الطاسة مكانها» and I resend.
- Left out on purpose: wet towels, flour, baking soda, extinguisher types (sources vary or do not mention them); the Egyptian fire number (no official page opened today, so «اتصلوا بالمطافي»). NFPA's page would not load.
- Line 5 («من كوباية مية») follows the fire services' demonstrations, where a small amount of water is used; no quantity is claimed. Lines 1 (that it is the first thing people do) and 10 are Peek's commentary.
