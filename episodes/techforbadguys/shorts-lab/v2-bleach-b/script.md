# Studio B: bleach Short, script v1

Egyptian Arabic, Peek reporting to his commander. 123 words. Status: work stopped by the manager on 2026-11-28 before the voice was recorded; this script was never timed against a take (the other studio's takes ran long, so expect to trim about fifteen words for a one-minute Short).

## Narration

1. يا فندم.. لقيت مصنع غاز سام على كوكب الأرض. المكان: الحمّام. المديرة: ماما.
2. يوم الجمعة. المهمة: التواليت لازم يلمع.
3. وعقيدة ماما: لو منظف واحد بينضّف.. يبقى تلاتة هينضّفوا تلات مرّات.
4. فبتعمل كوكتيل: كلور.. ومنظف تواليت.. ورشّة خل، للبركة.
5. بس الكلور مع منظف التواليت أو الخل.. بيطلّع غاز الكلور. ومع النشادر اللي في بعض منظفات الإزاز.. غاز سام تاني.
6. كحّة. عين بتحرق. ونَفَس مش طالع.
7. المهمة نجحت يا فندم: التواليت بيلمع. وماما.. في البلكونة.
8. وده التصرّف الصح: لو حصل، اطلع فوراً لهوا نضيف. ولو النَفَس تعبان.. اتصل بالإسعاف، وقول لهم خلطت إيه.
9. والقاعدة: الكلور ما يتخلطش بأي منظف تاني. منظف واحد في المرّة، تشطف بميّة كتير، والشباك مفتوح.
10. توصيتي يا فندم: نأجّل الغزو. الكوكب ده فيه ماما.. ومعاها كلور.
11. Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.

## The jokes

- Hook, rule of three: a toxic gas factory on Earth / the place: the bathroom / the manager: Mama.
- Mama's creed: if one cleaner cleans, three clean three times.
- The cocktail: bleach, toilet cleaner, and a splash of vinegar "for the blessing".
- Reversal: mission succeeded, the toilet shines, and Mama is on the balcony (which is also the correct action and leads into the advice).
- Callback button: postpone the invasion; this planet has Mama, and she has bleach.

## Sources

- Bleach + acid gives chlorine gas; acid products include toilet bowl cleaners and vinegar; bleach + ammonia gives toxic chloramine gases; ammonia is in some glass and window cleaners; symptoms: coughing, shortness of breath, burning and watery eyes (Washington State Department of Health): https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/contaminants/bleach-mixing-dangers
- If inhaled: leave the area immediately and breathe fresh air; with trouble breathing, get medical help (US National Capital Poison Center): https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas
- Rinse thoroughly before another product, allow time between products, keep ventilation; if exposed, fresh air, call emergency services and say which products were used (London Fire Brigade): https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/dangerous-chemicals/cleaning-products/
- Lines 1 (the "factory"), 2, 3, 7 and 10 are Peek's commentary, not facts. No brand names, no phone number, no treatment beyond fresh air and calling an ambulance.
