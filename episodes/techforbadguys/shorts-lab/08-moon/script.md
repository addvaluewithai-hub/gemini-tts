# لو الأرض كورة سلة.. القمر يبقى فين؟ أبعد مما تتخيل 🏀 #Shorts

**English title:** If Earth were a basketball, where is the Moon? Farther than you think

Format: Works with the sound off (08 moon). Look: clean scale model on a dark floor. Large Arabic text lines carry the story; the narration is light.

Each line gives: the narration, then **TEXT** (the on-screen line, at most 6 words), then the picture.

## Narration

1. يا فندم، لو كوكب الأرض كورة سلة.. القمر يبقى كورة تنس، بعيد عنها سبعة متر.
   **TEXT:** «الأرض كورة سلة.. والقمر فين؟»
   [Dark floor, top view: a basketball painted as Earth at the left edge, a tennis ball as the Moon far at the right, empty floor between; Peek tiny with a tape measure]
2. البشر دايماً بيرسموا القمر لازق في الأرض.
   **TEXT:** «في الرسومات: لازق فيها»
   [A textbook-style drawing slides in: Moon right next to Earth]
3. الرسمة دي غلط يا فندم.
   **TEXT:** «غلط»
   [The drawing gets crossed out and slides away; back to the dark floor]
4. الأرض عرضها حوالي اتناشر ألف وسبعمية كيلو. دي كورة السلة.
   **TEXT:** «الأرض = كورة سلة»
   [Close on the basketball-Earth, a width line across it]
5. القمر عرضه أكتر من ربعها بحاجة بسيطة. دي كورة التنس.
   **TEXT:** «القمر = كورة تنس»
   [The tennis ball-Moon placed beside the basketball to compare sizes]
6. المسافة بينهم في الحقيقة: تلتمية أربعة وتمانين ألف كيلو.
   **TEXT:** «المسافة: ٣٨٤ ألف كيلو»
   [The tennis ball starts rolling away across the dark floor]
7. يعني تلاتين كورة سلة ورا بعض.
   **TEXT:** «٣٠ كورة سلة ورا بعض»
   [Thirty ghost basketballs line up one by one between the two balls]
8. سبعة متر يا فندم. أوضة كبيرة.. فاضية.
   **TEXT:** «٧ متر.. فاضيين»
   [Wide top view: basketball, 7 m tape measure on the floor, tennis ball; Peek walking the distance]
9. ومحطة الفضا اللي البشر عايشين فيها؟ بعيدة عن كورة السلة أقل من سنتي.
   **TEXT:** «محطة الفضا: أقل من ١ سم»
   [Extreme close-up of the basketball surface: a tiny dot hovering just above it]
10. سنتي واحد.. قصاد سبعة متر. <short pause> وده أقرب جار عندهم.
   **TEXT:** «١ سم.. قصاد ٧ متر»
   [Pull back to the full floor: the dot by the basketball, the lonely tennis ball far away]
11. Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.
   [Sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell]

## Count

WORDS_LINE The narration is deliberately at the light end; the ten TEXT lines alone tell the story with the sound off.

## Sources

- Earth's equatorial diameter is 12,756 km: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/
- The Moon's radius is about 1,740 km (so about 3,480 km wide, 27% of Earth, "less than a third of the width of Earth"); it is on average 384,400 km away; "30 Earth-sized planets could fit in between Earth and the Moon": https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
- An NBA basketball is 75 cm in circumference (about 24 cm across): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_(ball)
- A tennis ball is 6.54–6.86 cm across (about 28% of the basketball, matching the Moon's 27%): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_ball
- The International Space Station orbits about 400 km above Earth: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Space_Station
- My arithmetic: 30 × 24 cm = 7.2 m («سبعة متر»); 400 ÷ 12,756 × 24 cm = 0.75 cm («أقل من سنتي»).
- **Changed from the brief:** nothing in the model (basketball, tennis ball, about 7 m). I added the space station at under 1 cm as the closing number.

**EN:** If Earth were a basketball, the Moon would be a tennis ball seven metres away, and the space station less than a centimetre up. Agent Peek measures it out.
