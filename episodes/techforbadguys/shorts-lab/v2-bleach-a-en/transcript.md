A toxic gas is being made in your bathroom. No. Not that one. Saturday morning. The American mom declares war on the bathroom. Her doctrine: one cleaner, clean. Two cleaners, cleaner. Three? The toilet ascends. But bleach plus toilet bowl cleaner, or vinegar, or some glass cleaners... makes toxic gas. Coughing. Burning eyes. Chest pain. And Mom? Proud. She thinks that's the smell of clean. That's not clean. That's an ALARM! The rule: never mix bleach with any other cleaner. Ever. Rinse well between products. Open a window. That's another report. How many cleaners are in your bathroom right now? Drop the number.
