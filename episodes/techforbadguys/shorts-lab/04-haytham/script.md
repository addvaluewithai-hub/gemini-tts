# اتحبس في بيته سنين.. وخرج بعلم الضوء: ابن الهيثم 🔦 #Shorts

**English title:** Confined to his house for years, he came out with the science of light: Ibn al-Haytham

Format: A life in a minute (04 haytham). Look: story scenes like a small film; Peek appears only in the first and last shots. No text on screen.

## Narration

1. يا فندم، فيه بشري اتحبس في بيته سنين.. وخرج منه بعلم الضوء.
   [Old Cairo room, a man in a turban at a desk, one beam of light through a small window; Peek small in the corner opening a file]
2. اسمه ابن الهيثم. اتولد في البصرة حوالي سنة تسعمية خمسة وستين.
   [Young scholar among books and geometry drawings, a river city behind him]
3. حسب الرواية، قال إنه يقدر ينظّم فيضان النيل.. فحاكم مصر بعت جابه.
   [A messenger hands him a sealed letter; a boat sailing toward Egypt]
4. راح أسوان، بص على النهر.. وفهم إن خطته مش هتنفع.
   [He stands tiny before the huge Nile, his rolled plan drooping in his hand]
5. ودي مشكلة.. لإن الحاكم ده ماكانش بيحب كلمة «ماعرفتش».
   [A stern ruler's silhouette on a throne; the scholar swallowing]
6. فبيتقال إنه عمل نفسه مجنون.. واتحبس في بيته لحد ما الحاكم مات، سنة ألف وواحد وعشرين.
   [He acts wild-eyed before guards; then a door closes on him at home]
7. وفي السنين دي كتب «كتاب المناظر».. سبع مجلدات عن الضوء.
   [Inside the room: he studies the light beam, writing; seven volumes stack up]
8. قبله، ناس كتير كانوا فاكرين إن العين بتطلّع شعاع.
   [An eye shooting rays outward at a candle, drawn like an old manuscript]
9. هو أثبت العكس: الضوء بييجي من الحاجة.. ويدخل العين.
   [The rays reverse: light bounces off the candle into the eye]
10. وبعدها بميات السنين، Galileo و Kepler كانوا لسه بيرجعوا لكتابه.
   [The book travelling across a map to later scholars with telescopes]
11. يعني يا فندم، البشر قفلوا على واحد أربع حيطان.. فشرح لهم هما بيشوفوا إزاي.
   [The door opens, light floods out of the room; Peek small, closing the file]
12. Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.
   [Sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell]

## Sources

- Born c. 965 in Basra, died c. 1040 in Cairo; by one account summoned by the Fatimid caliph to regulate the Nile flood, planned a dam at the site of today's Aswan Dam, saw it was impractical; "is said to have been forced into hiding until the caliph's death in 1021"; "Legend has it that Alhazen feigned madness and was kept under house arrest during this period. During this time, he wrote his influential Book of Optics"; Book of Optics, seven volumes, written 1011–1021; first to correctly prove vision is intromissive (light enters the eye) rather than extramissive; frequently cited by Galileo, Descartes, Kepler, Huygens: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_al-Haytham
- Hedged where the source hedges: «حسب الرواية» (the Nile offer) and «بيتقال» (feigned madness, house arrest). The script says «سنين», not a number, because the source gives no start date for the confinement. Britannica's page was blocked tonight (403).
- Kept neutral: the ruler is «حاكم مصر», unnamed; no religion, no politics. Line 5 is Peek's understatement, not a quote.
- Nothing changed from the brief.
