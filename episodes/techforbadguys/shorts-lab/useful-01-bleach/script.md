# الكلور مع منظف تاني عمره ما نضّف أحسن.. أمال إيه؟ ☠️ #Shorts

**English title:** Mixing bleach with other cleaners never cleaned better. So what does it do?

**What the viewer can do after watching:** use one cleaning product at a time, rinse well with water before using another, keep the window open, never mix chlorine bleach with toilet cleaner, vinegar or ammonia; and if it happens, get out to fresh air at once and call an ambulance if breathing is hard.

Format: useful Short 01. The drawn scene fills the screen; Peek has three full-screen studio shots (lines 1, 7, 11).

## Narration

1. يا فندم، البشر بيخلطوا الكلور بمنظف التواليت عشان الحمام ينضف أكتر.. فبيطلع لهم غاز سام.
   [PEEK: studio, full screen. He holds a bleach bottle in one hand and a toilet-cleaner bottle in the other, arms stretched wide apart, deadpan]
2. المنطق بتاعهم بسيط: منظف قوي، زائد منظف قوي.. يبقى نضافة أقوى.
   [SCENE: a mother in rubber gloves in a small bathroom, door and window shut; she pours bleach into the toilet, then reaches for a second bottle; a "1 + 1" bubble over her head]
3. الكيميا ليها رأي تاني.
   [SCENE: close on the toilet bowl: the two liquids meet and a yellow-green cloud starts to rise]
4. الكلور مع أي حمض، زي منظفات التواليت أو الخل، بيطلّع غاز الكلور.
   [SCENE: the bleach bottle beside a toilet-cleaner bottle and a vinegar bottle (unbranded); a plus sign, then the yellow-green cloud]
5. ومع النشادر، اللي في بعض منظفات الإزاز، بيطلّع غاز سام تاني.
   [SCENE: the bleach bottle beside a glass-cleaner spray (unbranded); a second, paler cloud]
6. والنتيجة: كحة، عين بتحرق، ونَفَس مش طالع.. في حمام مقفول.
   [SCENE: the mother coughing, eyes watering, hand on her chest; the cloud filling the closed bathroom]
7. وهي كل اللي كانت عايزاه.. إن القاعدة تلمع.
   [PEEK: studio, reaction. He looks at the two bottles, then slowly puts one down very far from the other]
8. لو حصل: اخرج فوراً لهوا نضيف. ولو النَفَس تعبان، اتصل بالإسعاف وقول لهم إنت خلطت إيه.
   [SCENE: she is out on the balcony breathing, bathroom door wide open behind her; then her phone at her ear, the two bottles visible in the doorway]
9. والقاعدة يا فندم: منظف واحد بس في المرة. تشطف بمية كتير قبل ما تستعمل التاني. والشباك مفتوح. والكلور ماينخلطش بأي منظف.
   [SCENE: three clear beats: one bottle alone on the floor; a bucket of water rinsing the bowl; the bathroom window open with fresh air arrows; then the bleach bottle alone with a "no mixing" sign]
10. يعني البشر بيخاطروا بصدرهم.. عشان بقعة.
   [SCENE: the clean, ventilated bathroom; one tiny stubborn stain winking on the bowl]
11. Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.
   [PEEK: studio, he salutes with one bottle only; then sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell]

## Sources

- "Don't mix bleach with ammonia, acids, or other cleaners"; bleach + ammonia gives toxic chloramine gases; bleach + acid gives chlorine gas; acid products include vinegar, some glass and window cleaners, toilet bowl cleaners, drain cleaners; ammonia is found in some glass and window cleaners; symptoms: coughing, breathing problems, burning and watery eyes, chest pain (Washington State Department of Health): https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/contaminants/bleach-mixing-dangers
- "Mixing bleach with an acid forms chlorine gas. If you inhale chlorine gas, leave the area immediately and breathe fresh air"; if you have trouble breathing or other serious symptoms, seek medical attention; the page's real case is a woman who used an acid toilet cleaner and then a bleach cleaner in her bathroom (US National Capital Poison Center): https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas
- "rinse surfaces thoroughly before using a different cleaning product", "allow time between using different products", "make sure there is good ventilation when cleaning"; if exposed: "leave the area immediately and get fresh air", call emergency services "and tell emergency services which products were being used" (London Fire Brigade): https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/dangerous-chemicals/cleaning-products/
- Left out on purpose: any brand name (I could not source what a named brand contains, so the script says «منظف التواليت»); any treatment beyond fresh air and calling for help; Egyptian emergency numbers (no official page for the ambulance number could be opened today, so it says «اتصل بالإسعاف»). CDC's bleach page was blocked (403).
- Lines 2, 7 and 10 are Peek's commentary, not facts.
