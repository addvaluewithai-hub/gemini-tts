# غاز سام في الحمّام.. لأ، مش اللي في بالك ☠️ #Shorts

**Studio A, version 2.** Topic: mixing chlorine bleach with other bathroom cleaners.

**What the viewer can say after watching:** bleach is never mixed with any other cleaner (toilet cleaner, vinegar,
glass cleaner); rinse well between products and open the window; if it happens, get out to fresh air at once, and if
breathing is hard call an ambulance and tell them what was mixed.

**Core message (one plain line, line 7):** «الكلور ما يتخلطش بأي منظف تاني. أبداً.»

**Narrative strategy:** a spy's field report. The hook is a double-take (a toxic gas made in the bathroom; "not the
one you are thinking of, sir"), the middle is the Egyptian Friday cleaning ritual told as a military campaign, the
turn is the gas, the rule is read like an order, and the ending pays off the pride in a shining toilet and calls
back the hook.

## Pacing notes

- Lines 1–2: slow and grave, a full beat of silence, then the quick flat aside. The silence belongs to the picture.
- Lines 3–4: building, like a war correspondent; «تلاتة؟» up, then the last phrase hushed and awed.
- Line 5: the tone drops. Slower, lower, stress on «غاز سام».
- Line 6: three hammer blows, a beat, then appalled deadpan.
- Line 7: the only shout in the piece.
- Lines 8–9: clipped, official, slower. No jokes inside the instructions.
- Lines 10–11: warm and dry again; a tired sigh before the callback.

## Facts and sources

- Bleach mixed with an acid gives off chlorine gas; acid products include toilet bowl cleaners, vinegar and some glass
  and window cleaners. Bleach mixed with ammonia gives toxic chloramine gases; ammonia is in some glass and window
  cleaners. Symptoms: coughing, shortness of breath, watery and burning eyes, chest pain, nausea. (Washington State
  Department of Health: https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/contaminants/bleach-mixing-dangers)
- If you inhale it, leave the area immediately and breathe fresh air; with trouble breathing or serious symptoms, get
  medical help. (US National Capital Poison Center: https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas)
- Rinse surfaces thoroughly before using a different product, keep good ventilation; if exposed, get fresh air, call
  emergency services and tell them which products were used. (London Fire Brigade:
  https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/dangerous-chemicals/cleaning-products/)
- Line 5 says «غاز سام» for both gases (chlorine and chloramine) and «بعض منظفات الإزاز», as the sources do.
- Lines 1–4, 6 (second half), 7, 10 and 11 are Peek's commentary, not facts. No brand, no phone number, no treatment
  beyond fresh air and calling an ambulance.
