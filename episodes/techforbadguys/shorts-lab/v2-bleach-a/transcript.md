## Plain text (English)

Sir.. the humans manufacture a toxic gas in the bathroom. No, sir.. not the one you're thinking of. Every Friday, the Egyptian mother declares war on the bathroom. And her doctrine is simple: one cleaner cleans. Two clean more. Three? The bathroom is born again. But bleach with toilet cleaner, or vinegar, or some glass cleaners.. gives off a toxic gas. A cough. Burning eyes. A hurting chest. And she? Proud. She thinks it's the smell of clean. That's not the smell of clean.. that's an alarm! The rule: bleach is never mixed with any other cleaner. Ever. Rinse well between one cleaner and the next, and open the window. And if it happens: get out to fresh air at once. And if breathing is hard, call an ambulance and tell them what you mixed. And the toilet? It will shine anyway. And she'll stand there watching it.. as if it got into medical school. As for the gas you were thinking of.. that's another report. Peek.. end of report, sir.
