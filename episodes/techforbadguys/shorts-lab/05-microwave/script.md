# حلويات ساحت في جيب مهندس.. فاتولد الميكروويف 🍫 #Shorts

**English title:** How does a microwave heat food? It began with a sweet melting in a pocket

Format: How it works in three steps (05 microwave). Look: cutaway diagram, steps 1-2-3. Step numbers and icons on screen.

## Narration

1. يا فندم، البشر بيسخّنوا أكلهم بجهاز بدأ بحتة حلويات ساحت في جيب مهندس.
   [Cutaway microwave oven; inset: an engineer's coat pocket with a melted candy bar dripping]
2. سنة ألف تسعمية خمسة وأربعين، مهندس اسمه Percy Spencer كان واقف قدام جهاز رادار شغّال.. وحسب الرواية، الحلويات اللي في جيبه ساحت.
   [Engineer beside a radar set with wave arcs; icon: candy bar melting; "1945"]
3. مازعلش على الجيب. جاب دُرة.. وبقت فشار.
   [Icon: corn kernels popping in front of the radar tube]
4. طيب الجهاز بيشتغل إزاي؟ تلات خطوات.
   [Clean cutaway of the oven, three empty numbered slots 1 2 3]
5. واحد: قطعة جوّاه اسمها magnetron بتطلّع موجات مابتتشافش.
   [Big "1"; the magnetron lights up at the side, waves coming out]
6. اتنين: الموجات محبوسة في صندوق معدن، بتخبط في حيطانه وترجع على الأكل.
   [Big "2"; arrows bouncing off the metal walls onto a plate of food]
7. تلاتة: المية اللي جوّه الأكل بتتهز مع الموجات مليارات المرات في الثانية.. والهزّ ده بيبقى حرارة.
   [Big "3"; zoom into the food: water molecules spinning back and forth, a thermometer rising]
8. يعني الجهاز مابيسخّنش الأكل يا فندم.. بيخلّي الأكل يسخّن نفسه.
   [The plate steaming while the oven walls stay cool-blue]
9. وأول واحد اتباع كان طوله متر وتمانين ووزنه تلتمية وأربعين كيلو.. <short pause> ماكانش بيتحط على رخامة المطبخ.
   [A giant 1947 oven towering over a tiny modern one; icons "1.8 m" and "340 kg"]
10. Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.
   [Sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell]

## Sources

- "According to legend, one day while building magnetrons, Spencer was standing in front of an active radar set when he noticed the candy bar he had in his pocket melted"; he then experimented with food, including popcorn kernels: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Spencer
- Raytheon filed the patent for Spencer's microwave cooking process on 8 October 1945; microwaves from a magnetron fed into a metal box "from which it had no way to escape"; the oven makes polar molecules (water) in food rotate and produce heat (dielectric heating); the cooking chamber is microwave-reflecting; household ovens use 2.45 GHz (2.45 billion cycles a second, hence «مليارات المرات في الثانية»); the 1947 Radarange, the first sold, was almost 1.8 m tall and weighed 340 kg: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
- Hedged: the pocket story is «حسب الرواية», as the source calls it a legend; the source says "candy bar" with no brand, so the script says «حلويات» only. Line 8 is a simplification of "the heat is produced inside the food". The last half of line 9 is a joke.
- Nothing changed from the brief.
