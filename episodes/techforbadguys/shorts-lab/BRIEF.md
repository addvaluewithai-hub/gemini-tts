# Shorts lab 01: ten standalone Shorts, ten styles

Owner's direction (2026-11-26): Shorts are no longer cut from the long videos. Each Short stands alone: one useful
thing in about a minute, told by Agent Peek as part of his report on Earth. These ten are a test: ten formats and
looks, so the owner can choose how the channel's Shorts will look. **Due in R2, reviewed: Fri 27 Nov 06:30 UTC
(08:30 Cairo).** The owner decides at 09:00 Cairo.

## For all ten

- Egyptian Arabic, Peek's voice (Puck on Vertex, the usual direction). 40–60 seconds, at most ~130 words.
- Stands alone: no "full report", no part numbers, no chapter chip from an episode.
- The first frame already shows the thing the title promises; the first sentence is the hook.
- One idea. A beginning, a turn, an end. The last line lands (a twist, a number, a question), then a two-second
  sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell, and Peek's sign-off line (the writer writes one, the same for all ten).
- Portrait 1080x1920, about −15 LUFS. A thumbnail with no words.
- Files: R2 `shorts-lab/NN-key-ar.mp4` and `shorts-lab/NN-key-thumb.jpg`.
