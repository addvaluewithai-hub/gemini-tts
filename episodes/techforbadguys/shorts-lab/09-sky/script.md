# ليه السما زرقا؟ وليه بتحمرّ ساعة الغروب؟ 🌅 #Shorts

**English title:** Why is the sky blue, and why does it turn red at sunset?

Format: The commander asks (09 sky), two voices. Look: a transmission, the commander's signal on a screen, Peek answering. Speech bubbles. The commander (deep voice) only asks short questions.

## Narration

1. Peek: يا فندم، سما الكوكب ده زرقا.. مع إن ضوء شمسهم أبيض.
   [Peek at his transmitter under a bright blue sky, a white sun; the commander's signal waveform on a screen; Peek's speech bubble]
2. القائد: وليه زرقا؟
   [The waveform pulses; commander's bubble with a question mark]
3. Peek: الضوء الأبيض يا فندم هو كل الألوان مخلوطة في بعض.
   [A white beam splitting into rainbow colours on Peek's small display]
4. Peek: ولما بيدخل الهوا، بيخبط في جزيئات الغاز ويتبعتر في كل ناحية.
   [Light rays hitting tiny air molecules and scattering]
5. Peek: والأزرق موجته قصيرة.. فبيتبعتر أكتر من باقي الألوان، ويملا السما كلها.
   [Short choppy blue waves bouncing everywhere, filling the sky; long red waves passing straight]
6. القائد: وليه بتحمرّ ساعة الغروب؟
   [Waveform pulses; the sky behind Peek starts shifting to orange]
7. Peek: ساعة الغروب الشمس بتبقى واطية، فضوءها بيعدّي في هوا أكتر عشان يوصل.
   [Diagram: low sun, a long path through the atmosphere to a tiny observer]
8. Peek: الأزرق بيتبعتر ويضيع في السكة.. واللي بيوصل هو الأحمر والأصفر.
   [Blue waves dropping out along the long path; red and yellow arriving]
9. القائد: يعني نفس السبب؟
   [Waveform pulses, short]
10. Peek: نفس السبب يا فندم. اللون اللي بيملا لهم النهار.. هو اللي بيضيع منهم في الغروب.
   [Split frame: blue noon sky and red sunset side by side]
11. القائد: والبشر عارفين ده؟
   [Waveform pulses]
12. Peek: أغلبهم لأ يا فندم. <short pause> بيصوّروه وخلاص.
   [Peek deadpan; behind him rows of humans holding phones up at the sunset]
13. Peek: Peek.. انتهى التقرير يا فندم.
   [Sign-off card: EARTH REPORT, the bell]

## Count

103 Arabic words spoken (sign-off included), about 46 seconds at Peek's usual pace.

## Sources

- Sunlight looks white but is all the colours of the rainbow; it is scattered in all directions by the gases and particles in the air; "Blue is scattered more than other colors because it travels as shorter, smaller waves"; "As the Sun gets lower in the sky, its light is passing through more of the atmosphere to reach you. Even more of the blue light is scattered, allowing the reds and yellows to pass straight through to your eyes": https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/blue-sky/en/
- Line 12 («أغلبهم لأ») is Peek's joke, not a statistic.
- Nothing changed from the brief.
