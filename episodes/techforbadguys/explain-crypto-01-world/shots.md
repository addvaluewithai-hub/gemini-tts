# Crypto part 1, world picture: the shot list

Every board beat is one wordless illustrated shot (`video/sets/<scene>.js`), drawn with `sets/kit.js` in the world of
the bank set (`sets/bank.js`): the same town at dusk and at night, the same bank, the same brown book with the blue
and pink dots, the blue house (Ali), the pink house (Mona), a teal house (Sami). People named in the script are
objects: Satoshi is an empty hooded chair at a glowing laptop, Hal Finney a desk with a lamp and a mug, Laszlo a
green house with an antenna. Host beats (`close`, `host`, `wide`) are unchanged. `host left/right` = Peek leans in
from that side, so that lower corner is kept empty. Times are seconds from the start of the beat.

Things that travel through the episode: **the book** (a ledger, 100/50 → 70/80 for blue/pink), **the bank** (stone
front, `BANK`), **the blue and pink houses**, **gold coins** (money), **the printing press** (money from nothing),
**the hooded chair** (Satoshi), **the calendar page** (every date), **the pizza boxes**.
