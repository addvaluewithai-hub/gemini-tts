# Crypto part 4, world picture: the shot list

One wordless shot per board beat (`video/sets/c4<scene>.js`), drawn in the world of parts 1 to 3 (`sets/kit.js`,
`bank.js`, `props.js`, `c2props.js`, `c3props.js`, and part 4's props in `sets/c4props.js`). Host beats unchanged;
`host left/right` keeps that lower corner clear. Times come from the Vertex take (`node sets/beats.cjs`); put each action
on its word.

Things that travel through the episode: **the price mountain** (a night ridge drawn as the price line: every peak is a
real dated peak, every valley its fall; a small gold coin rides on it like a cable car), **the balance** (part 2's),
**the gold bar and the coin side by side** (digital gold), **the four buyers** as four houses (a gambler's red, a
fleeing blue with a suitcase, a teal one sending abroad, a violet tower for institutions), **the newspaper** that keeps
printing obituaries, **the leverage seesaw** (your coins on a long plank lifted by the exchange's borrowed coins),
**the whale** (a big gold whale swimming under the price line), **the fund building** (glass, with a wall between you
and the coin), **the three report cards** (money's three jobs from part 1: pay, price, save). Every price carries its
year on the calendar page; no price is shown without a date, and nothing reads as a forecast or a tip.
