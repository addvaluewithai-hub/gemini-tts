# Crypto part 6, world picture: the shot list

One wordless shot per board beat (`video/sets/c6<scene>.js`), drawn in the world of parts 1 to 5 (`sets/kit.js`,
`bank.js`, `props.js`, `c2props.js`, `c3props.js`, `c4props.js`, and part 6's props in `sets/c6props.js`). Host beats
unchanged; `host left/right` keeps that lower corner clear. Times are seconds from the start of the beat
(`node sets/beats.cjs`, scratchpad `c6/beats.txt`); each action sits on its word. No real person's likeness: the
richest man is a tall dark tower with a megaphone, the president is a podium with a flag-coloured coin, the co-creator
is a little house that drives off in a used car. Every figure carries its date; nothing reads as advice.

Things that travel through the episode: **the book** (the public ledger of parts 1 to 3), **the framed picture**
(an NFT: a gold frame with a pixel ape or a picture, joined to one row of the book by a gold thread), **the copier**
(part 1's copy problem: a picture duplicating), **the dog coin** (a gold coin with a dog face), **the price mountain**
(part 4's ridge, here as small spiky meme mountains that rise and collapse), **the rug** (a red carpet under a crowd
of little houses, yanked away), **the pump** (a bicycle pump inflating a coin like a balloon), **the zoo of coins**
(cages with odd coins, from part 5's "garden"), **the gravestones** (dead coins), **the central-bank vault** (a stern
grey building with a coin of its own), **the quantum lock** (the key from part 3 facing a glowing atom), and the
**calendar page** for every date.
