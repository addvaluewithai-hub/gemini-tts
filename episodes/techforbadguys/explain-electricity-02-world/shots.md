# Electricity part 2 of 4 (made and delivered), world picture: the shot list

Same world, props and rules as part 1 (`../explain-electricity-01-world/shots.md`): the file's own teal-and-navy
night (`P` from `sets/elec.js`), the teal house and the orange house, electrons as cyan balls with a minus, gold for
light and "look here". Nothing in Arabic on screen; numbers, years, units (W, MW, %), names in Latin letters
(FARADAY, THREE GORGES), symbols. No people: Faraday is a name plate, a lamp-lit workbench and a book. Peek leans in
from the side on beats marked host left/right; keep that lower corner clear.

New things that travel through this part: **the generator** (a horseshoe magnet spinning inside a copper coil, with
the electrons in the coil going back and forth); **the turbine** (a giant fan of blades on the generator's shaft);
**the kettle** (from part 1) as the unit of power; **the sun** as the source behind almost everything.
