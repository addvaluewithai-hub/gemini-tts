## Script

### intro

Sir. The sixth and final report in the cryptocurrency file. Today: the hype, and the law. Last report, we learned anyone can make a coin in minutes. So humans made twenty million. Some with a picture, some with a dog, one with a president's name. I'll tell you which lived, which died, and what governments did. Then my verdict on the whole file.

### s1

In 2021, the humans discovered the ledger could record who owns anything at all. So they recorded pictures. One digital picture sold for sixty-nine million dollars. And the first tweet in Twitter's history sold for almost three million. A year later its owner listed it. The best offer: two hundred and eighty dollars. Celebrities bought cartoon apes for hundreds of thousands. Why? The same old reason: someone else will pay more. The buyer wasn't buying the picture. He bought a line saying it was his. The picture itself... anyone can copy. Remember the copy problem from the first report? Still here. Two years later, a study found ninety-five percent of those collections... were worth nothing. But the idea came back in a suit: shares and bonds on a ledger. That's what big exchanges are building now.

### s2

Now the strangest cage in the zoo. In 2013, two programmers made a coin to mock the whole thing. With a dog on it. By 2021, the joke was worth about ninety billion dollars. Still worth billions. The richest human on the planet would post one line about it... and the price went up. Another line... down. One creator sold his share early... and bought a used car. He understood it best. Then came sites that make you a coin in under a minute, no code at all. Even a president launched one in his name, three days before taking office. It reached seventy-four dollars. In November 2026: a dollar and change. Down ninety-eight percent.

### s3

Two games here. The rug pull: the maker collects the money and vanishes. Pump and dump: buy early, make noise, and when you get in... they get out. Either way, one person always loses: the latecomer. The final count: twenty million coins made by the end of 2025. More than half... dead. A coin here lives shorter than a carton of milk. They're hunting the next.

### s4

Their governments don't agree. China banned it in 2021. Europe allowed it under a law, from the end of 2024. America is still arguing with itself. So the law changes country by country. Anyone should check their own country's law first. The one thing they all agreed on: taxes. And the governments' answer: a digital currency from the central bank. Over a hundred countries are studying it. Almost the same technology. The exact opposite idea: not a ledger with no guard. A guard... holding the whole ledger.

### s5

After six reports, my final assessment for Command. First, what worked. One: the invention is real. A ledger with no owner, running seventeen years, and nobody has changed an old line. Two: for the first time, something digital... that can't be copied. Three: the digital dollar is really used, especially where banks let people down.

### s6

What didn't work. One: nobody buys bread with it. Two: the billion with no bank account? Most still have none. What brought people in was the phone... not bitcoin. Three: the middleman they fled came back three times: an exchange, a fund, and a digital-dollar company. Four: around it, they built the planet's biggest casino. In fairness: the technology is seeping into the old system. Banks and exchanges are testing ledgers like this.

### s7

Some questions remain. New machines called quantum computers might one day break the keys. In March 2026, Google researchers said that would take machines twenty times smaller than thought. Programmers are preparing new locks. A race... not over yet. And one from the second report: when the mining prizes run out... who pays the guards? The bottom line, sir: the invention is brilliant. And humans used it like every brilliant thing: a quarter to solve a problem... three quarters to place bets. Do I recommend Command buy? Agent Peek isn't authorized to give investment advice. Not even to his planet.

### s8

The last two words from the Human Dictionary. Altcoin: any coin that isn't bitcoin. All twenty million of them. And airdrop: free coins handed out to reel you in. Nothing's free on this planet.

### s9

Summary of the whole file, sir. One: money is a ledger, and bitcoin gave it to everyone. Two: the key is ownership, and what's lost stays lost. Three: the price is a bet, not a promise. Four: the ledger now runs programs, and its biggest success was the digital dollar. And five: most of what came after... is noise. One last warning: all of this is explanation, not advice. If you don't understand it... don't put your money in it. The crypto file is closed. Six reports. This planet has plenty more that needs explaining. This has been Earth Report. Agent Peek. End of transmission.
