# Crypto part 5, world picture: the shot list

One wordless shot per board beat (`video/sets/c5<scene>.js`), drawn in the world of parts 1 to 4 (`sets/kit.js`,
`bank.js`, `props.js`, `c2props.js`, `c3props.js`, `c4props.js`, and part 5's props in `sets/c5props.js`). Host beats
are unchanged; `host left/right` keeps that lower corner clear. Times are seconds from the start of the beat
(`node sets/beats.cjs`, scratchpad `c5/beats.txt`); put each action on its word.

Things that travel through the episode: **the book** (parts 1 to 4) that now grows a little **screen and gears** on its
cover once it runs programs; **the vending machine** (the smart contract: a coin slot, a glass front with one item, a
green/red condition light, a chute; its code shows as coloured bars on its side panel, the same bars as part 1's code
case); **the staking vault** (a glass box of locked coins with a flame grate under it that burns a cheater's stake);
**the token press** (a tiny desk stamp that pops out new coins in any colour); **the digital dollar** (a green coin
with `$1`, a real banknote standing behind it on a thread); **the freeze button** (a big blue snowflake button on the
stablecoin company's desk); **the lending pool** (a round pool of coins with pipes in and out, a collateral scale on
its edge); the laptops on the planet (the copies); the hooded chair is NOT used (Ethereum's founder is not drawn; a
young laptop at a desk with a calendar stands for him). Every figure carries its date on a calendar page or tag.
